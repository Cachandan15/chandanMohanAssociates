About the firm

Chandan Mohan & Associates is a Chartered Accountancy firm based in Anupgarh, Rajasthan, founded in March 2023 by CA Chandan Aggarwal. The firm partners with business owners, professionals, and organisations to bring clarity, compliance, and financial discipline to their operations.

Our services span audit and assurance, direct and indirect taxation, regulatory and statutory compliance, accounting support, and financial advisory. We combine sound technical knowledge with a practical understanding of the challenges businesses face every day, helping clients make informed decisions while meeting their compliance responsibilities on time.

The firm is led by CA Chandan Aggarwal, who qualified as a Chartered Accountant in 2021. He began his professional journey with PwC Service Delivery Center, Kolkata, where he gained approximately 15 months of experience in a structured, high-quality professional environment. Building on this foundation, he established Chandan Mohan & Associates with the aim of providing accessible, dependable, and personalised professional services to clients in Anupgarh and beyond.

At Chandan Mohan & Associates, we believe that professional advice should be timely, transparent, and tailored to each client’s needs. Our approach is rooted in integrity, attention to detail, and a long-term commitment to our clients’ financial and regulatory well-being.
